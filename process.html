The 6-Step Process

01 Days 1 - 3

Understand

02 Days 4 - 7

Strategize

Architect The 30-Day Growth Blueprint

We establish your 8-part content engine, define monthly themes, craft hook angles, and schedule our first 30-day production timeline.

03 Days 8 - 14

Create

Precision Scripting & Visual Craft

We script, design, edit, and write copy for your Reels, carousels, and stories. We deliver simple shot-list checklists for any smartphone video clips needed from your staff.

04 Ongoing Execution

Publish

Multi-Channel Distribution

Content is scheduled and published at peak engagement windows across Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile with active direct-response CTAs.

Outcome: Consistent, active brand presence without taking a single minute of your daily operational schedule.
05 Monthly Rhythm

Measure

Transparent Analytics & Tracking

We aggregate real platform telemetry: tracking profile visits, outbound WhatsApp taps, save rates, and genuine inbound inquiries.

Outcome: An honest monthly report showing exact business signals rather than superficial vanity fluff.
06 Monthly Review

Optimize

Hypothesis Testing & System Refinement

We review the data together on our strategy call, retire underperforming angles, and scale what is driving real engagement and inquiries.

Outcome: A compounding marketing system that becomes sharper, smarter, and more effective every single month.
