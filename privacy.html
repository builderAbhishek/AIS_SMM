Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025 • Alag Innovative Solutions

1. Introduction

Alag Innovative Solutions ("AIS", "we", "us", or "our") operates the website for the AIS Social Growth System. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you request a social media audit, book a strategy call, or communicate with our team.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us via our web forms, including:

  • Full Name and contact details (WhatsApp number, telephone, and email address).
  • Business details (Business name, industry vertical, location, website URL, and social media handles).
  • Operational nuances and marketing goals communicated during strategy questionnaires.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the submitted information exclusively for:

  • Conducting your requested manual 7-point social media diagnostic audit.
  • Coordinating and scheduling your 30-minute strategy call.
  • Tailoring content frameworks and package proposals suited to your local business.
  • Responding directly to your inquiries via WhatsApp, telephone, or email.

We never sell, rent, or lease your business data or contact lists to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Security & Retention

We maintain industry-standard security protocols to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Client project files and strategic documents are retained solely for the duration of the consulting relationship.
